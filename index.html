How grades are computed

  • Items inside a component are averaged with equal weight (e.g. every quiz counts the same). A blank total counts as 100.
  • Components you haven't filled in are left out, so the grade shown is your current standing on what's been graded.
  • GWA = Σ(grade × units) ÷ Σ units. PE counts toward the GWA here (the official UP GWA leaves it out). NSTP is shown but not counted.
  • Honors lines: per semester, University Scholar ≤ 1.45 and College Scholar ≤ 1.75; at graduation, Summa ≤ 1.20, Magna ≤ 1.45, Cum Laude ≤ 1.75. Other conditions (full load, no failing grades) also apply.
  • Bio 110 lab uses the Lab 7 weights (exams 60, quizzes 20, worksheets and reports 15, performance 5). Chem 18's percent-to-grade table isn't in the syllabus, so it borrows the Bio scale; edit it under the course's grading scale once you know the real one.
